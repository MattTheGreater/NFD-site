Suggested Reading

Where the argument comes from.

Books and authors that shaped our thinking on marriage, the family, and the law. This list will grow.

Taken Into Custody: The War Against Fathers, Marriage, and the Family

Stephen Baskerville

How family law turned the dissolution of marriage into an instrument of state power over the private household.

The Sexual State

Jennifer Roback Morse

The ideological project behind no-fault divorce, contraception, and the redefinition of the family, and the public cost it carries.

The Case for Marriage

Linda J. Waite and Maggie Gallagher

The social-science case that marriage, as a durable commitment, makes people healthier, wealthier, and better off than the alternatives.

The Unexpected Legacy of Divorce

Judith Wallerstein, Julia Lewis, and Sandra Blakeslee

A twenty-five-year study of children of divorce, following the consequences long after the decree.

Them Before Us: Why We Need a Global Children’s Rights Movement

Katy Faust and Stacy Manning

The case for ordering family policy around the rights of children rather than the preferences of adults.

Disassembly Required: A Memoir of Midlife Resurrection

Beverly Willett

A first-hand account of fighting an unwanted unilateral divorce, from one of our allies.
